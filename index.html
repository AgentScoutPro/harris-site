Meet the Owner

Built by Greg.
Backed by Service.

Greg Harris discovered his passion for construction early, working alongside local builders through high school. That passion took a detour through U.S. Army Special Operations. The schools were brutal. The missions were real.

What came out the other side was something no classroom teaches: the ability to assess any situation, build a solid plan, and execute without excuses.

"Everything I've been through has made me who I am today. I always try to do the right thing, I'm very upfront, and I strive to do my best no matter what it is. I truly love what I do."

Greg Harris, Founder and Owner

Today Greg runs Harris Home Improvement alongside his wife Allison, his partner for over 11 years. When he is not on a job site you will find him on the golf course, with family, or wrangling his three French Bulldogs.
