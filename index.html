Meet the Owner

Built by Greg.
Backed by Service.

Greg Harris didn't stumble into construction. He discovered it early, working alongside local builders through high school and developing a genuine passion for designing and building things that last.

That passion took a detour through one of the most demanding paths a person can walk. U.S. Army Special Operations. The schools were brutal. The missions were real. What came out the other side was something no classroom teaches: the ability to assess any situation, build a solid plan, and execute without excuses.

"Everything I've been through has made me who I am today. I always try to do the right thing, I'm very upfront, and I strive to do my best no matter what it is. I truly love what I do."

Greg Harris, Founder & Owner

Today Greg runs Harris Home Improvement alongside his wife Allison, his partner for over 11 years who helped build the business structure while working her own career in healthcare. When he's not on a job site, you'll find him on the golf course, with family, or wrangling his three French Bulldogs.
