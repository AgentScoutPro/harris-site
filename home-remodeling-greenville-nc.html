Common Questions

Home Remodeling FAQ

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Greenville NC?
A basic bathroom refresh with new vanity, toilet, flooring and fixtures typically runs $5,000 to $12,000 in the Greenville area. A full gut renovation with a tiled walk-in shower, new layout and custom vanity can range from $15,000 to $35,000 or more. Harris Home Improvement provides detailed, itemized estimates before any work begins.
Do you handle permits for home renovations in Pitt County?
Yes. We coordinate all required permits with Pitt County and the City of Greenville for any work that requires them. You do not need to deal with the building department on your own.
How long does a room renovation take?
A standard room renovation takes one to two weeks. A full bathroom gut can take two to three weeks. Room additions and whole-home renovations are scoped and scheduled individually based on your project.
What areas near Greenville do you serve for home remodeling?
Harris Home Improvement serves all of Pitt County plus Beaufort County, Craven County, Lenoir County and Wilson County. Key cities include Greenville, Winterville, Ayden, Farmville, Washington, New Bern, Havelock, Kinston and Wilson.
