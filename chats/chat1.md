what format of video is best for a video walk through of a project for a project they completed

## Assistant

For a website video walkthrough, the best format is:

**MP4 (H.264)** — hands down the most compatible option for web use.

Key specs to aim for:
- **Codec:** H.264 (video) + AAC (audio)
- **Resolution:** 1080p (1920×1080) — good quality without massive file size
- **Frame rate:** 30fps is plenty for a walkthrough
- **Bitrate:** 5–8 Mbps for 1080p
- **File size target:** Under 50MB for web embedding, ideally under 20MB

**Why MP4/H.264:**
- Plays natively in every browser, no plugins needed
- Easy to compress without losing visible quality
- Works on phones, tablets, and desktops
- Compatible with every video hosting platform

**Hosting recommendation:** Don't embed the raw MP4 directly on the page — it will slow load times significantly. Instead upload to **YouTube** (unlisted if you want it private) or **Vimeo** and embed the iframe. This gives you:
- Fast streaming via their CDN
- Auto quality adjustment based on connection speed
- No impact on your page load speed

If they're shooting on an iPhone, the native `.mov` format records in H.264 or HEVC — just export/share as MP4 before uploading and it'll be ready to go.
