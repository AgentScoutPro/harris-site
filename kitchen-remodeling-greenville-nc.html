Common Questions

Kitchen Remodeling FAQ

How long does a kitchen remodel take in Greenville NC?
Most full kitchen remodels in the Greenville area take between two and six weeks depending on scope. A cabinet and countertop refresh may be done in a week. A full gut renovation with new layout, flooring, electrical and plumbing coordination can take four to six weeks. Greg gives you a firm schedule before work starts.
How much does kitchen remodeling cost in Pitt County NC?
Kitchen remodel costs in Eastern NC vary widely based on scope. A mid-range kitchen update with new cabinets, countertops and appliances typically runs $15,000 to $40,000. Full gut renovations with layout changes can range from $40,000 to $80,000 or more. Harris Home Improvement provides free, detailed estimates so you know exactly what you are getting before signing anything.
Do you handle permits for kitchen renovations in Greenville?
Yes. Harris Home Improvement coordinates all required permits with the City of Greenville and Pitt County. We handle the paperwork so you do not have to.
