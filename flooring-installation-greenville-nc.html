Common Questions

Flooring FAQ

How long does flooring installation take in Greenville NC?
Most flooring projects in the Greenville area take one to three days depending on square footage and floor type. LVP and laminate installs are often complete in a single day. Hardwood with sanding and finishing takes two to three days minimum.
What type of flooring is best for Eastern NC homes?
LVP is extremely popular in Eastern NC due to its waterproof properties, humidity resistance and durability. Hardwood remains the premium choice for living areas. Tile works best in bathrooms and kitchens. We help you pick the right product for each room of your home.
Do you remove old flooring before installing new floors?
Yes. Harris Home Improvement handles full demo and disposal of your existing flooring as part of the project. We inspect the subfloor before any new material goes down.
What areas do you serve for flooring installation?
We install flooring throughout Pitt County (Greenville, Winterville, Ayden, Farmville), Beaufort County (Washington, Chocowinity), Craven County (New Bern, Havelock), Lenoir County (Kinston) and Wilson County.
