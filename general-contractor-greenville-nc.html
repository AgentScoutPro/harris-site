Meet Greg Harris

Built by a Green Beret

Greg Harris grew up working construction alongside local builders in Eastern NC. He discovered early that he had a passion for building things that last. After high school, life took him on a different path when he joined the United States Army and entered Special Operations.

The training was brutal. The missions were real. What came out the other side was a man who could assess any situation, build a solid plan and execute without excuses. Those same principles guide every project Harris Home Improvement takes on today.

Greg runs the business alongside his wife Allison, his partner for over 11 years, who helped build the company's structure while working her own career in healthcare. When he is off the job site you will find him on a golf course, spending time with family or wrangling his three French Bulldogs.

"Everything I have been through has made me who I am today. I always try to do the right thing, I am very upfront and I strive to do my best no matter what it is. I truly love what I do."

Greg Harris, Owner
